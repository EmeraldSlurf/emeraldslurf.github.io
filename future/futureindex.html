good undergrad schools

Johns Hopkins University: Known for extensive biomedical research and clinical opportunities.

Harvard University & Stanford University: Offer major research funding, teaching hospital access, and structured pre-health advising.

Duke University & University of Pennsylvania: Strong ties to major academic medical centers and research labs.

how to pick school

GPA vs. rigor: Introductory science courses at highly selective colleges can be fiercely competitive, and medical school applications look closely at your overall GPA.

Cost: Because medical school adds years of tuition and debt, saving on undergraduate costs—especially through in-state public options or financial aid—can be a major advantage.

Experience access: Look for nearby hospitals, clinics, or labs where you can build clinical hours and research experience.
